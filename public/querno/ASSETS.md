# Querno portfolio assets

Sourced from the local Querno project (https://querno.vercel.app), October 2026:

- `icon.svg`: `site/public/favicon.svg`, the entangled block Q.
- `editor-{light,dark}.webp`, `library-{light,dark}.webp`: `site/public/shots/*.jpg`, captured from the real app at 2×, resized to 2000 px wide and encoded as WebP.

Fonts in `app/fonts/`, under the GUST Font License:

- `lmroman*.otf`: the unmodified OpenType files from Querno's `site/public/fonts/`.
- `latinmodern-math-subset.woff2`: Latin Modern Math 1.959 from GUST (https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/lm-math), subset with fontTools to Latin letters, digits, math italics, π, ∑, ∞ and basic operators, keeping its MATH table.
