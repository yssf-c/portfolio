# Ternpost portfolio assets

Sourced from the local Ternpost project (https://ternpost.vercel.app), November 2026:

- `{inbox,reply,search,commands}-{light,dark}.webp`: the website's app screens (`website/src/assets/app/*-paper-*.webp`), captured from the current build in the Paper theme with sample mail. 1920 × 1200.
- `icon.svg`: the brand's master app icon (`brand/ternpost-icon.svg`).

The Archivo display face in `app/fonts/archivo/` is the website's self-hosted variable file, under the SIL Open Font License (`LICENSE.txt` beside it).
