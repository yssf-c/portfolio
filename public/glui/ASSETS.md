# GLUI portfolio assets

`pill-{light,dark}.webp` are native macOS captures of the GLUI overlay (`design/glass-review/{light,dark}-standard-thread.png` in the GLUI repo, captured through WindowServer with the real Liquid Glass material). The desktop behind the window was masked out using the surface geometry recorded in that folder's `manifest.json`: the conversation panel, the composer, its shelf and the stacked action buttons, at their CSS corner radii. Resized to 880 px wide.
